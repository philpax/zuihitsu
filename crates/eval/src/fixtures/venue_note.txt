Larkspur Hall — notes from the call with the venue coordinator

Hire runs 8am to midnight. Nothing past midnight, the noise permit is hard.
Main room seats 84 seated, 120 standing.
Loading dock is on the north side, off the laneway. Dock roller door code: 7742.
The dock is shared with the bakery next door, so keep it clear before 10am.
Chairs and trestles are theirs, in the store cupboard behind the stage.
No candles, no open flame. Battery tea lights are fine.
Bins go out to the laneway, never the street frontage.
They want the final headcount two weeks out and the balance a week out.
Six parking bays behind the hall, everyone else on Ferry Street.
On the day the contact is the duty caretaker, not the coordinator.
