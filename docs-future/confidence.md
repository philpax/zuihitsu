# Confidence register

Normative chapters specify the proposed successor architecture. Their claims do not have equal support. This register records the evidence status, unresolved decisions, and gates for each load-bearing claim.

## Status vocabulary

The following table is the canonical mapping across evidence, coverage, and lifecycle vocabulary. A row may combine one evidence status with one lifecycle status. Coverage status describes only whether a known failure is representable. It never upgrades evidence.

| Requested distinction | Canonical status | Permitted aliases and combinations | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| observed evidence | `Observed` | May combine with `Corroborated`, `Genesis-blocking`, or `Stage-gating` | Established by direct measurement, code, tests, or current-system documentation. |
| verified evidence | `Verified` | `Verified, corroborated` when independent sources agree | Checked against a fetched primary source by the adversarial verification pass. |
| corroborated evidence | `Corroborated` | May qualify `Observed` or `Verified` | Multiple independent sources agree. |
| single-source evidence | `Single-source` | May combine with `Open experiment` | Rests on one study or system and is unreplicated. |
| decided policy | `Decided` | May combine with any lifecycle class | An operator or architecture decision. Evidence can support the premise, never prove the decision. |
| design synthesis | `Synthesis` | May combine with `Genesis-blocking`, `Stage-gating`, or `Safely deferred` | An inference by this design that the cited sources do not themselves make. |
| unvalidated hypothesis | `Unvalidated hypothesis` | `Open` in older chapter tables | A specified claim with named alternatives that lacks decisive evidence. It must have one unresolved-register row unless explicitly settled or declined. |
| structural solution | `Closed structurally` | Coverage-only; never an evidence status | The successor representation makes the named historical failure unrepresentable. |
| design answer without validation | `Answered in design, not yet validated` | Coverage-only | A mechanism is specified but its behaviour or economics has not passed its gate. |
| genesis blocker | `Genesis-blocking` | Lifecycle class | The successor cannot safely begin without this record shape or rule. |
| gated extension | `Stage-gating` | `Gated extension` in README and overview | A policy or capability remains disabled until its named gate passes. |
| safely deferred capability | `Safely deferred` | May later become `Stage-gating` | Genesis records every required input, so later addition does not reinterpret history. |
| open experiment | `Open experiment` | A safely deferred capability with no activation commitment | Not part of the committed architecture until its experiment and gate pass. |
| settled scope exclusion | `Declined` | Not unresolved | Deliberately excluded. The row names the future condition that would reopen it. |

## The verification pass

Two adversarial passes ran over the research report before the design was taken up ([`research/2026-07-24/verification/`](research/2026-07-24/verification/)). Of its load-bearing cited claims: 29 confirmed against fetched primary sources, 5 corrected, 0 unsupported, 1 unreachable. Every future-dated citation was fetched directly rather than judged from memory, and all resolved to real sources supporting the claims as reported.

The five corrections are folded into the chapters. They were: the identification of provenance polynomials with justification labels relabelled as this design's own synthesis rather than a claim the source makes; the conservatism about subjective logic extended from its fusion operators to its evidence-count mapping; the severance description tightened so re-derivation is a record-time pass rather than part of the fold; the drift figures for the long-running accumulator made precise, being a monotonic decline in newly promoted claims rather than a cliff; and the causal story for forced-choice elicitation tempered to distinguish omission variance from content variance.

## Claims deliberately not made

- That the design closes the eleven surveyed failures. It closes six structurally and answers five in design without validation. See [`coverage.md`](coverage.md).
- That any benchmark number here is a target. The one composite memory benchmark surveyed was found by independent audit to have a materially wrong answer key and a judge that accepted most intentionally wrong answers. Component success is measured by structural oracles against this system's own log, not by a self-reported score.
- That autonomy means no human attention. It means exception-triggered attention with a queue that shrinks per fact as the store grows.
- That the neural writer is verified for truth. The critics check well-formedness, typing, consistency, and whether an extracted value is traceable to a source locator. That check establishes groundedness rather than truth. A well-typed falsehood can still pass at write time. Runtime faithfulness checking remains unsolved.
