# Redesign the ontology against its surveyed failures: the Statement keystone and a verified neural/symbolic seam

## Problem

The dated failure survey (`docs/ontology-failures/2026-07-23.md`) records eleven places where the current ontology fails in practice, gathered while building the maintenance passes and from a full survey of the testing instance's live knowledge base. Read together they are not eleven independent bugs; they are the symptoms of one architectural choice and one architectural absence.

The choice is that **a fact is a prose sentence**. An entry is a prose blob anchored to exactly one memory, so every downstream mechanism — dedup, consolidation, arbitration, temporal placement — must re-derive structure from prose, repeatedly, nondeterministically, and fallibly. A structural question ("what did this person build?") has no structural answer. That is failure class 1, and classes 2, 3, and 8 follow from it directly: a multi-participant event shatters into per-subject copies because a sentence has one subject (one implementation-event was recorded three times in a single session); a relation cannot carry "worked at X from 2019 to 2021" because a bare edge has no interval, so it becomes prose; belief has no credence because a sentence has nowhere to put one.

The absence is a **verified seam between the neural writer and the symbolic store**. The model is the sole writer of structure, and the guards check authority and visibility but never truth (class 11). The sharpest datum in the survey is class 10: a corroboration-capture behaviour moved from roughly 6% to 75% of eligible cases on a single scaffold sentence (the `attestation_hidden_confirmation` eval, 1/16 capture before the change, 6/8 after). That a load-bearing data-capture behaviour swings that far on wording is the welding failure in miniature — the interface between live neural judgement and the symbolic store is behavioural, tuned by phrasing, and re-tunes itself unpredictably across model generations.

The welding question is therefore the organising frame of this redesign: **where should the neural/symbolic boundary sit, what should cross it, and what disposes of what crosses?** The convergent-evolution survey states the thesis in one line: every durable symbolic system that worked had a curator; zuihitsu removed the curator and put an LLM in its seat. The repair is not a better prompt. It is to stop asking the neural writer to be the curator.

This issue proposes the shape of the redesign for a future agent's genesis. There is no migration constraint. It is the actionable distillation of the accompanying research report (adversarially verified: 29 load-bearing claims confirmed against fetched primary sources, 5 corrected, 0 unsupported); the report holds the full argument and the citations, and its lane files hold the evidence. What follows states each element with its motivating failure class, not its full defence.

## The proposed shape

**The keystone: one first-class Statement object.** Today three concepts — ContentEntry, Attestation, and scattered provenance metadata — carry the weight of "a thing was asserted". Unify them into one reified, addressable object, the **Statement** (the Wikidata statement crossed with TypeDB role-typing and the neo-Davidsonian event shape). It carries a structured claim (a typed `(subject, relation, object)` triple or a reference to an Event node with role-edges), a prose gloss retained beside the structure and subordinate to it, provenance qualifiers kept exactly one level deep, bitemporal timestamps plus a valid-time interval, a credence, a transmission principle, and a derivation record with an assumption stamp. This single object dissolves classes 3, 8, and 11 at once and gives forgetting and the inter-agent boundary a shared substrate. Attestation stops being a separate concept: a Statement with several `told_by` tellers *is* "a fact a set of tellers stand behind", each endorsement carrying its own transmission principle and retraction authority. The honest cost, stated up front: every fact is now a small graph, not a row.

**Events and roles (class 2).** A multi-participant happening is one Event node with role-edges from a small closed universal role set (`agent`, `patient`/`theme`, `instrument`, `time`, `place`, `recipient`, and a handful more), never per-subject prose copies. Everything else is an attribute on the Event, not another role — PropBank's numbered roles above ARG0/ARG1 and FrameNet's frame-specific inventories are unteachable to a single-utterance writer, so the universal set stays small and the gloss preserves the surface form when a role is uncertain. The three-times-recorded implementation event becomes one Event; the re-mention resolves to it as a structural NOOP.

**Relations: attribute-bearing, interval-scoped, schema-evolving (classes 3, 6 / #42).** A relation is promoted from a bare edge to a first-class Statement with a validity interval (supersession by window-closing, never deletion), provenance, credence, and declared domain and range on the relation *definition* so a reversed or mis-typed edge is caught as a teachable error at write time. Vocabulary evolves by **deprecate-and-alias**, event-sourced: a mis-coined relation is deprecated and aliased to a canonical one, reads resolve aliases transitively, and the "coined four ways" drift collapses to four aliases of one canonical relation at read time. A free-text `context` side channel keeps the closed enum honest. Two independent production systems (bound's edge-graph normalization RFC, LifeOS) reinvented exactly this fix and both needed a migratable enum, not an immutable one.

**Identity: revocable composite identity behind the substrate wall (classes 5, 7 / #94, #104).** Drop union-find `class_id` as *the* identity structure — transitivity is the amplifier that turns one wrong merge into a corrupted class. Keep stubs-forever, but make each `same_as` merge a first-class, credence-bearing **assumption** rather than a boolean transitive union: graded (a subjective-logic opinion), contextual (the Gamma/Lambda split separates same-for-recall from same-for-disclosure), and revocable. Every derived event carries a small assumption stamp of the revocable merges in force, and severance is a fold filter — `MergeSevered` voids stamped events and re-derives from the separated stubs, so "the world as if never merged" is computed, not forensically reconstructed. Collective entity resolution on the graph's relational structure (participation, acquaintance, placement) is the passive signal, structurally expensive to forge where recited attribute overlap is not. All of it lives *below the substrate wall* (the Soar/ACT-R data/metadata wall): the agent sees one resolved handle per person and never chooses between `person/priya`, `person/priya@chat`, and `person/priya@forum`, killing #104's bare-handle minting and handle-equality testing.

**Belief: credence from evidence, never from the model's mouth (class 8).** Every Statement carries a subjective-logic opinion (belief, disbelief, uncertainty, base rate), derived from **evidence counting** — the number and independence of attestations and the tellers' track records — not from the LLM verbalising a probability, which is overconfident, saturates to coarse values, and shifts with the elicitation protocol. Where the model must judge, it is quantised to a coarse ordinal (suspected / likely / confirmed). Trust discounting weights an attestation by teller reliability; independent-versus-dependent fusion is the recitation defence at the belief layer. Belief revision is non-prioritised, replacing episodic prose-note arbitration and persist-as-a-flag contradiction handling. The surface stays ordinal and provenance-anchored: "three people told me this, one usually unreliable" beats "confidence 0.72".

**Time: the schedule/description split and typed values (classes 4, 9 / #103).** Split the single occurrence field on the iCalendar model into a **descriptive occurrence** (VEVENT `DTSTART`, kept — a fact describing another bot's cron legitimately recurs here), a **deontic Task** (VTODO `DUE`, present only when the agent itself must act), and a **Trigger** (VALARM, what the scheduler arms on). A pure description has no Trigger and can never fire, so the other bot's daily cron and the namesake's 1789 birth year can never wake the agent. Dates, durations, and recurrences become first-class typed values backed by `jiff::civil::Date`/`jiff::Span` end to end, with anchor-aware durations (killing the fixed-30-day month) and recurrence constructors that cannot encode the `BYMONTHDAY=31` trap. A tractable subset of Allen's interval algebra generalises `BeforeAfter` so orderings can be inferred.

**Memory typology: four kinds with distinct lifecycles (#58, #59, #74).** Memory kind becomes a first-class type distinction because each kind has different lifecycle, authority, retrieval, and decay rules. **Semantic** (today's ContentEntry, now a Statement): durable, credence-bearing, visibility-gated, distilled. **Episodic** (#74): the conversation and tool log as an automatic, read-only, provenance-marked fallback tier, never edited, never distilled, recency-decayed, and never laundered into asserted truth. **Procedural** (#58): executable Luau indexed by a description embedding, decayed by invocation recency and frequency (ACT-R base-level activation), not calendar age. **Working/scratchpad** (#59): persistent-but-transient, private, outside the visibility model entirely; reflection promotes or discards. Retrieval and decay move off ad-hoc `exp(-dt/tau)` and embedder-geometry constants onto ACT-R activation plus distribution-relative thresholds recomputed on `EmbeddingModelChanged` — the class-9 fix. Bulk ingestion (#44) is handled natively: a long document lands as semantic Statement clusters linked by `part_of`/`summarizes` role-edges plus an episodic source layer retaining each span as the gloss.

**Privacy, provenance, and forgetting.** Keep and strengthen the posture model. The posture enum becomes the default library of a small closed vocabulary of **transmission principles**, each compiling to a deterministic predicate over (present set, log history) — contextual integrity's transmission principle promoted to first-class data. This lets confidences travel across the inter-agent boundary, makes the subject-guard a derived negative norm, and gives calendar and reminder flows a principled privacy story. `visible()` stays cheap, fail-closed, and deterministic; the zero-residue requirement is held as a non-interference invariant and `Public`-only distillation is declassification. The derivation record adopts the PROV shape, upgrading `produced_by` from "records model and template" to "records model, template, premises, agent, time, **and evidence and criteria**" — the specific class-11 gap — with a defeasible assumption/justification layer on top. Forgetting distinguishes **Retract** (a tombstone; content survives for audit; reversible) from **Forget** (crypto-shred or delete the payload; irreversible; the fact of forgetting itself audited via the retained hash-chained envelope). Erasure propagates through the derivation graph — the second independent argument, with identity severance, for the defeasible layer. Retraction authority is a lattice over {teller, subject, operator}, answering the subject-differs-from-author case; inter-agent claims enter as quotations, not assertions.

**The query surface stays handle-shaped.** Beside search, the agent gains first-class **structural reads** — "who did X build?" traverses `agent` role-edges, "when did she work at Y?" reads the relation's interval, "how do you know?" reads the derivation record. The richness lives entirely in the substrate; the surface exposes a small vocabulary of typed reads and the same teachable-error pedagogy, and the agent never speaks ontology-language.

## The welding architecture

The fact model is necessary but not sufficient: a typed store still fails if the neural writer fills it unverifiably. This is the core research question, and the architecture specifies the seam.

**A typed seam (class 1).** The agent sits on the proposal side. Its writes cross as typed structured proposals — a neo-Davidsonian Event with typed roles, a typed interval, a transmission principle, a relation instance against a typed schema — never an un-typed prose sentence. If the seam is typed, no downstream mechanism re-parses prose, because the structure was captured at write time. Adopt the DeepProbLog/Scallop/LLM-modulo doctrine as doctrine (not machinery): the neural half never gets to be the final authority on a structural question.

**Neural proposes, symbolic disposes: a bank of critics (class 11).** Auto-regressive LLMs cannot reliably self-verify, so replace "model judgement polices model-written structure" with critics that run at write time and can reject a write. **Hard critics** are sound, symbolic, and gating: type and domain/range checks, mutual-exclusion constraints (NELL's drift brake — a thing cannot be both a city and a person), audience-visibility invariants (no attestation wider than founding posture, now a write-time critic), `same_as`-authority checks, temporal well-formedness, and a duplicate-window NOOP that resolves a re-mention to an existing Statement rather than appending a copy. A violation is rejected with a teachable error — the existing pedagogy, now backed by a sound check. **Soft critics** are LLM-based, sampled-and-voted, non-gating, and reserved for genuine language judgements.

**Forced-choice elicitation (class 10).** Any behaviour whose failure is a silent correctness or safety failure moves off the prompt into a required schema field the turn cannot complete without filling — fact capture, visibility-posture setting, speaker resolution, temporal placement, the schedule-versus-description distinction, the wake-up decision. The 6%-to-75% *omission* swing collapses because capture is no longer a function of wording — but the variance relocates into the field's content, and a required field adds a junk-fill failure mode, so elicitation is paired with the hard critics, an explicit "nothing to record" option, and content-level evaluation. The scaffold teaches principles and taste (which tolerate wording drift); the harness enforces load-bearing steps (which do not). This is not free: the Constraint Tax can suppress tool-calling or degrade reasoning in some models, so constrain the load-bearing writes and leave the generative parts free, adjudicated per behaviour by eval.

**Provenance computed with the conclusion.** Provenance is a structural artefact of derivation, computed with the conclusion, not attached post hoc — evidence, criterion, and assumption stamp. NELL's coupling insight is the strongest under-used idea in the survey: require agreement between two independent signals (embedding geometry and model judgement, or two independent tellers) before a fact is promoted from candidate to settled. The candidate/promoted boundary is an explicit, inspectable credence and threshold, not a vibe.

**In-block LLM calls (#100).** The in-block `llm` call is the welding seam reified. It is a record-at-call-time non-deterministic activity (Temporal's determinism model): the first execution emits a `ModelCalled`-style event carrying prompt and response into the log; replay consumes the recorded response with no model call. It is exposed primarily as native, schema-constrained functions (`llm.extract_event`, `llm.classify_visibility`, `llm.resolve_speaker`, `llm.yes_no`) — the delivery vehicle for forced-choice elicitation — with a freeform `llm.complete` as a deliberative, non-committing escape hatch only. Every `llm`-derived write still passes the hard critics.

**Drift management.** An autonomous accumulator drifts unless something outside the neural loop watches it. The ontology is the primary drift brake (typed, mutually-exclusive, constraint-checked structure is what a candidate write can violate); the rest is detection — canary facts (known-true, known-false, known-private, re-probed over a simulated lifetime), periodic re-derivation audits (assert dedup/consolidation does not oscillate, re-run after `EmbeddingModelChanged`), and distribution-relative thresholds plus ACT-R activation replacing magic cosine constants. Canaries and audits are necessary, not sufficient: they see seeded regions and stability, and are weakest against gradual precision decline in the newly-written tail (NELL's actual drift shape), which is what the agreement-before-promotion coupling exists to brake.

## Staged milestones

Each stage names what it unblocks, its gating eval, and its main risk. Stages 0-1 are the load-bearing bet; the later stages are independently valuable and can be reordered against evidence.

**Stage 0 — Statement/Event spike against recorded logs.** Model the Statement and Event/role shape and the first hard critics (type, domain/range, duplicate-window NOOP), and replay them `rejudge`-style over recorded real logs with no live model. *Unblocks:* confidence that the structure captures real utterances before committing the seam. *Gating:* extraction of the recorded three-copy event (seqs 436-440, 590-595) resolves to one Event with correct roles; the duplicate-window critic flags the re-filings. *Risk:* the universal role set proves too small or too large on real data.

**Stage 1 — Statement substrate, typed seam, and critics.** Build the Statement as the atomic write unit, the typed seam, and the hard/soft critic bank, with forced-choice elicitation for the load-bearing writes. *Unblocks:* everything downstream — every later stage writes Statements. *Gating:* the extraction-fidelity harness (gold-structure scenarios scored precision/recall, plus a faithfulness oracle that every structural write is entailed by some utterance) holds; the paraphrase-spread probe shows near-zero spread on captured field-content correctness (not mere capture presence, which a required field pins by construction). *Risk:* the Constraint Tax costs more than it buys on the target model — measured, not assumed.

**Stage 2 — time, Task/Trigger, typed values.** The iCalendar three-way split and jiff-backed typed dates, durations, and recurrences (#103). *Unblocks:* correct wake-up semantics; the temporal graph. *Gating:* a scenario where a dated *description* (a third-party cron, a historical date) never fires, and a genuine Task with a Trigger does. *Risk:* the Allen subset chosen is too weak to infer the orderings that matter.

**Stage 3 — memory typology.** Episodic fallback tier (#74), procedural Luau (#58), and the working scratchpad (#59), each with its own lifecycle. *Unblocks:* the "nowhere principled to live" issues; bulk ingestion (#44). *Gating:* episodic recall surfaces only on semantic miss and is never laundered into asserted truth; a bulk-ingested document is navigable and its extraction precision does not fall against a short-document baseline. *Risk:* the scratchpad-storage question (log-with-compaction versus side-table) is unresolved and could bloat replay.

**Stage 4 — revocable identity, assumption stamps, severance.** `same_as`-as-assumption, assumption-stamped derivations, the fold-filter severance, and collective ER on relational structure, all behind the substrate wall. *Unblocks:* zero-administration identity (#94); the #104 behaviour fix. *Gating:* a merge-then-sever scenario folds cleanly to "as if never merged"; the post-merge sibling-history relay (`a_merge_lands_and_memory_unifies`, today 0.30) recovers. *Risk:* the assumption-stamp representation and crumble thresholds are open and need real data.

**Stage 5 — credence and revision.** Subjective-logic opinions from evidence counting, trust discounting, independent-versus-dependent fusion, non-prioritised revision. *Unblocks:* the recitation defence at the belief layer; principled contradiction handling. *Gating:* two recited (dependent) attestations produce no spurious confidence gain; a low-credibility teller does not overturn a high-credibility one. *Risk:* the exact credence shape is a live lane disagreement; the specific fusion operator needs validating against its critics before it is relied upon.

**Stage 6 — transmission principles and Forget.** Postures promoted to first-class transmission-principle data; Retract/Forget with derivation-graph propagation; the retraction-authority lattice; inter-agent claims as quotations. *Unblocks:* cross-boundary confidences; a lawful erasure story. *Gating:* the zero-residue non-interference invariant holds under a Forget that propagates through derived conclusions; `Public`-only distillation never leaks an `Attributed` or private fact. *Risk:* the combinatorial audience-context blowup if audiences are ever anything richer than predicates over the present set.

**Stage 7 — longitudinal drift harness and the autonomy queue.** Lifetime-spanning scenarios with canary re-probes and re-derivation audits; the operator exception queue (#15/#20). *Unblocks:* the zero-administration endgame — exception-triggered touch backed by drift detection. *Gating:* a canary flip, an audit oscillation, or a post-`EmbeddingModelChanged` structural shift raises exactly one alarm and no false positives over a clean run. *Risk:* drift is a longitudinal property a single-turn scenario structurally cannot see, so the harness itself is novel work.

## Related issues

The proposal speaks to the open issues as follows. **#7** (persistent-memory-agent survey) is the landscape the research lanes built on. **#15** (self-write authority) and **#20** (autonomous activity) are answered by `Agent`-authority self-observations behind the wall, with exception-triggered operator touch replacing continuous curation. **#42** (relation schema evolution) is deprecate-and-alias. **#44** (bulk ingestion) is semantic Statement clusters plus an episodic source layer and the decision-time axis. **#58** (procedural), **#59** (working scratchpad), and **#74** (episodic) are the three kinds of the four-kind typology. **#94** (autonomous identity, reversibility) is revocable composite identity, assumption-stamped severance, tiered reversibility, and challenge-response for the irreversible tier. **#100** (in-block neural judgement) is record-at-call-time `llm` activities, primarily schema-constrained. **#103** (typed time) is first-class jiff-backed values. **#104** (merged-identity behaviour failures) is the substrate wall.

## Autonomy and reversibility

The organising principle, endorsed and generalised from #94: **tiered reversibility applied to every derived conclusion**. Split effects by whether severance can genuinely undo them.

**Revocable effects** — unified recall, shared inference, composite briefs, distilled descriptions, consolidations, link inferences, everything internal to the substrate — ride tentative, credence-bearing assumptions and are safe to be imperfect *because severance genuinely undoes them*: an assumption retraction voids the stamped derivations and re-folds the log. The Gamma/Lambda split licenses this for identity — same-for-recall rides the revocable tier without propagating same-for-disclosure.

**The genuinely irreversible set** is three things TMS is silent on because they are external effects: **outbound disclosure** (a confidence spoken across an identity or audience boundary — you can void a derived brief entry, but you cannot un-say it), **external side effects** (a message posted, an email sent, an action taken in the world), and **Forget** (crypto-shredding is irreversible by design). These stay gated on the hard bar: a completed challenge-response (#93) or evidence past a far stricter threshold for identity-boundary disclosure, and explicit operator or subject authority for Forget.

The operator exception classes, with NELL's expectation that this is a *small* queue and not continuous curation: identity-boundary disclosure the substrate cannot clear by relational evidence alone (rare, bounded by genuinely ambiguous cross-platform encounters); Forget requests where authority must be confirmed (rare, event-driven); drift alarms — a flipped canary, an oscillating audit, a post-`EmbeddingModelChanged` shift (episodic, detection-triggered rather than a standing duty); and hard-critic rejections that suggest a schema gap rather than an agent mistake (falling over time as the schema matures). The road not taken, named honestly: MemGPT/Letta give the model direct control over its own state via tool calls with no separate deterministic materialisation layer — a live, validated design. The redesign deliberately chooses checked-and-deterministic curation over model-curates-itself, precisely because class 11 flags the unverified writer, and the cost of that choice is more substrate machinery and less agent autonomy over its own salience.

## Open questions carried from the report

- **Working-scratchpad storage** (#59): log-with-compaction versus a side-table, trading replay-purity against log size. The lane leans log-with-compaction but flags it as its weakest recommendation.
- **The exact credence shape**: subjective logic (richest, best-motivated for the recitation defence, heaviest) versus a lighter (strength, confidence) pair or a categorical decision. The lanes disagree; the specific fusion operator, if any, needs validating against the "Can we trust subjective logic" critique before it is relied upon.
- **The Allen subset**: verification resolved the boundary — ORD-Horn is the maximal tractable subclass (Nebel & Bürckert, JACM 1995) — so the remaining question is only whether the simpler pointisable subalgebra already covers the anchors the agent writes.
- **The assumption-stamp representation**: a per-event set versus a shared assumption-environment table referenced by id — a storage-versus-fold-time trade.
- **Crumble and accretion thresholds** for tentative merges: an empirical tuning problem, because genuine same-person profiles also diverge.
- **Eager versus lazy structuring**: how much dedup judgement (the cheap ADD-versus-NOOP distinction) moves in-line at write time versus deferred to a maintenance pass, against the Constraint Tax.
- **The Constraint Tax itself**: how much schema-forcing the target model tolerates before tool-calling or reasoning degrades — measured per behaviour, not assumed.

## Fixed points (not up for redesign)

The redesign holds these invariant: the **append-only log as the sole source of truth with deterministic replay** (model and embedder calls at record time only); **privacy at least as strong as today** (per-fact audience postures, hidden-endorsement zero-residue semantics, the subject-guard); **teachable-error pedagogy** with an agent-facing surface that stays handle-shaped and simple, however rich the substrate becomes; and **deliberately unbounded scale**, with bulk ingestion of long documents handled natively rather than excepted.
