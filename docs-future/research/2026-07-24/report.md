# Designing an ontology against its failures: a research report for the zuihitsu redesign

This is the primary deliverable of a design-research program on zuihitsu's ontology. It reads the surveyed failures of the current ontology (`docs/ontology-failures/2026-07-23.md`) as the adjustment input, takes seven research lanes as its evidence base, and proposes a redesign of the fact model, relations, identity, belief, time, memory typology, privacy, provenance, and the neural-symbolic seam that welds the neural agent to the symbolic store. The proposal targets a future agent's genesis; there is no migration constraint.

Every load-bearing claim carries the citation the lanes gathered. Where a lane flagged a claim as unverified or drawn from a secondary source, the flag is preserved inline for the adversarial-verification pass that runs next. Where the lanes contradicted one another, the disagreement is recorded rather than smoothed over. Costs and open questions are stated plainly; an overclaimed proposal is worse than a hedged one.

The lane reports are the evidence appendix. This report summarises their findings (section 2) and points into them for detail; the design (sections 3 to 6), the failure cross-check (section 7), and the honest-costs accounting (section 8) are where the work is.

---

## 1. Problem statement: the welding question

zuihitsu is a neurosymbolic personal-agent harness. The symbolic half is an append-only event log — the sole source of truth, replayed deterministically, with model and embedder calls happening at record time only — materialised into a SQLite knowledge graph. The neural half is an LLM agent living in conversation, acting through a sandboxed Luau API. One instance is one agent is one log. The agent meets people across platforms, remembers what each said, and keeps confidences between them.

The survey names eleven failure classes. Read together, they are not eleven independent bugs; they are the symptoms of one architectural choice and one architectural absence.

The choice is that **a fact is a prose sentence**. Every downstream mechanism — dedup, consolidation, arbitration, temporal placement — must therefore re-derive structure from prose, repeatedly, nondeterministically, and fallibly. A structural question ("what did this person build?") has no structural answer. This is failure class 1, and classes 2, 3, and 8 are its immediate consequences: a multi-participant event shatters into per-subject prose copies because a sentence has one subject; a relation cannot carry "worked at X from 2019 to 2021" because a bare edge has no interval and the alternative is prose; belief has no credence because a sentence has nowhere to put one.

The absence is a **verified seam between the neural writer and the symbolic store**. The model is the sole writer of structure, and the guards check authority and visibility but never truth (failure class 11). The most telling datum in the whole survey is failure class 10: a corroboration-capture behaviour moved from roughly 6% to 75% of eligible cases on a single scaffold sentence (the `attestation_hidden_confirmation` eval, 1/16 capture before the change, 6/8 after). That a load-bearing data-capture behaviour swings that far on wording is the welding failure in miniature — the interface between live neural judgement and the symbolic store is behavioural, tuned by phrasing, and re-tunes itself unpredictably across model generations.

The welding question is therefore the organising question of the redesign: **where should the neural/symbolic boundary sit, what should cross it, and what disposes of what crosses?** Everything else — the fact model, the identity model, the belief model — is downstream of getting that seam right. The convergent-evolution survey (lane `survey-giants.md`) states the thesis in one line: every durable symbolic system that worked had a curator; zuihitsu removed the curator and put an LLM in its seat. The repair is not a better prompt. It is to stop asking the neural writer to be the curator.

## 2. Survey findings

Condensed from the seven lanes. Each subsection points into its lane for the full argument and citations.

### 2.1 Fact shape, relations, and schema evolution (lane `fact-shape.md`)

The entire knowledge-representation literature converges on the same skeleton: **a fact is a reified statement node, not a prose blob**. Davidson's 1967 move added an event variable so modifiers attach as separate conjuncts of a shared event; the neo-Davidsonian refinement (Parsons 1990) pulls the verb's own arguments out as thematic-role edges, so one event has many independently addressable role-edges ([Landman course notes](https://www.tau.ac.il/~landman/Online%20Class%20Notes/2%20ADVANCED%20SEMANTICS/8%20Neo-davidsonian%20event%20semantics.pdf)). This is the principled fix for "one event, many copies". The KR side arrives at the same primitive from the n-ary-relations direction: the W3C n-ary Note's two patterns are precisely zuihitsu's failure classes 2 and 3 ([W3C n-ary](https://www.w3.org/TR/swbp-n-aryRelations/)), and TypeDB implements it as a role-typed hypergraph with enforced role-filler types ([TypeDB](https://typedb.com/blog/the-case-for-a-structured-hypergraph); vendor-authored, architecture corroborated by [arXiv:2506.05626](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.05626)).

The most battle-tested real-world shape is the **Wikidata statement**: a first-class object carrying qualifiers, references, and a rank ([Wikidata data model](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Data_model)). Its hard-won discipline is to keep qualifiers exactly one level deep, because a qualifier modifying a qualifier makes scope ambiguous ([Wikidata Help:Qualifiers](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Help:Qualifiers), which states the rule directly; [arXiv:2304.03375](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.03375) for the surrounding analysis). Its cautionary note is that ranks are a crude three-value filter, not a credence ([Wikidata ranking](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Help:Ranking)).

The critical finding for the welding worry: **schema-guided extraction does not burden the LLM writer, it helps it**. Apple's ODKE+ reports 98.8% precision over roughly 19 million extracted facts by constraining extraction to per-type ontology snippets ([Apple ML Research](https://machinelearning.apple.com/research/odke); [arXiv:2509.04696](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.04696)); schema grounding materially reduces hallucination and reversed-relationship errors [the specific "~87% reduction / 85% of errors corrected" figures are from secondary summaries and flagged for verification — the direction is solid, the exact numbers are not]. So the class-1 fix (structure over prose) and the class-11 concern (unverified writer) are aligned, not opposed: structure is what makes the writer checkable.

Relation vocabulary should evolve by **deprecate-and-alias**, not immutability: a mis-coined relation is marked deprecated and aliased to a canonical one, reads resolve aliases transitively, and the four coinings of one relation collapse to four aliases of one canonical (the Wikidata "interested in -> field of work" bot-merge pattern, [Properties for deletion](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Properties_for_deletion)). Because zuihitsu is event-sourced, the mechanism is read-time alias resolution plus upcasting ([ScienceDirect empirical study of event-sourced schema evolution](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0164121221000674)), preserving the append-only fixed point. Domain/range on a relation turns misuse into a teachable error at coin time. (Verified: OWL's deprecation vocabulary is `owl:DeprecatedClass`/`owl:DeprecatedProperty`, and LinkML carries a `deprecated` slot with `deprecated_element_has_exact_replacement` — the deprecate-and-alias lifecycle has standard-vocabulary precedent.)

Keep the structured form **and** the source utterance together (Zep's episode subgraph, MemCube provenance-with-content), so the canonical structure is queryable and the original prose is retained for re-derivation and audit ([Neo4j Graphiti blog](https://neo4j.com/blog/developer/graphiti-knowledge-graph-memory/)).

### 2.2 Provenance, privacy, and forgetting (lane `provenance-privacy.md`)

Four independent literatures — PROV bundles, nanopublication pubinfo, named graphs, and CKR contexts — **all reinvented "make the assertion a first-class, addressable, context-scoped object"** ([PROV-DM](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/); [Nanopublication guidelines](https://nanopub.net/guidelines/working_draft/); [Named Graphs, WWW 2005](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1060745.1060835); [CKR](https://dkm.fbk.eu/technologies/theoretical-frameworks/ckr-contextualized-knowledge-repository/)). zuihitsu's attestation model got halfway there from the privacy side. The highest-leverage move is to unify attestation, provenance, and visibility into one object.

PROV supplies the derivation-record shape (entity/activity/agent, qualified derivation with a plan slot for the template), but it is descriptive, not defeasible — it records that an assumption was used, not that a conclusion's truth is contingent on it. That gap is exactly failure class 11 and is where an ATMS/justification layer is wanted. Nanopublications supply the retraction discipline: supersession and retraction are first-class signed events referencing a content-addressed prior, and who-may-retract is a signature check — but nanopubs leave open the case where the subject differs from the author, which a personal agent must answer with a retraction-authority lattice over {teller, subject, operator} ([Semantic micro-contributions, PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7959648/)).

Contextual integrity gives the cleanest formal statement of the privacy fixed point: privacy is appropriate information flow governed by a five-tuple whose **transmission principle** is a temporal condition over the trace, not a static tag ([Nissenbaum](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/228198982_Privacy_As_Contextual_Integrity); [Barth et al. 2006](https://nyuscholars.nyu.edu/en/publications/privacy-and-contextual-integrity-framework-and-applications)). An append-only log is uniquely well-suited to evaluate such conditions. zuihitsu's four postures are implicit transmission principles; promoting them to first-class data lets confidences travel across the inter-agent boundary, makes the subject-guard a derived negative norm, and gives calendar flows a principled privacy story. The zero-residue requirement is formally non-interference (Goguen-Meseguer), and `Public`-only distillation is declassification — a controlled downgrade [the non-interference/declassification framing is standard security theory; the lane flagged it lacks one canonical citation to attach].

Forgetting reconciles with append-only replay by distinguishing **Retract** (a tombstone; content survives for audit; reversible) from **Forget** (crypto-shred the payload or delete a forgettable payload; content gone; irreversible; the fact of forgetting itself audited via the retained hash-chained envelope). Replay is redefined as deterministic over the post-forget log ([Verraes crypto-shredding](https://verraes.net/2019/05/eventsourcing-patterns-throw-away-the-key/); [EventStore GDPR](https://docs.eventsourcingdb.io/best-practices/gdpr-compliance/)). Erasure must propagate through the derivation graph — the strongest single argument for the defeasible ATMS layer [the "prove a redaction occurred without revealing content" tamper-evident-log property is a synthesis of crypto-shredding plus append-only tombstones, flagged as lacking one canonical citation].

### 2.3 Identity, belief, and truth maintenance (lane `identity-belief.md`)

The whole field converged on the conclusion that **identity should not be a single hard equivalence**. `owl:sameAs` licenses unrestricted transitive substitution, which is far too strong for how the link is actually used ([Halpin et al., ISWC 2010](https://www.ibiblio.org/hhalpin/homepage/publications/ldow2010.pdf)); the transitive closure of 558M sameAs statements manufactured false equivalences, collapsing 177K distinct entities into single classes ([sameAs.cc](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/325529520_sameAscc_The_Closure_of_500M_owlsameAs_Statements)). **Transitivity is the amplifier**: a union-find `class_id` is a transitive-closure structure, so one wrong merge corrupts the whole class. Identity should be graded (a credence, not a boolean), contextual (the Gamma/Lambda split: indiscernibility-for-recall need not license propagation-for-disclosure, [sameAs survey](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1907.10528)), and revisable (a link you can retract before the closure fossilises it).

The recitation attack (#94) is formally a Fellegi-Sunter independence violation: probabilistic record linkage's arithmetic depends on field-agreement probabilities being independently generated, and recited facts are not independent evidence, so summing their weight double-counts one act of copying ([Fellegi-Sunter working paper](https://www.census.gov/content/dam/Census/library/working-papers/1993/adrm/rr93-12.pdf)). The defence is **collective entity resolution on relational structure** (Bhattacharya & Getoor, [ACM TKDD 2007](https://linqs.org/assets/resources/bhattacharya-tkdd07.pdf)): two references co-refer when their neighbours co-refer, and relational evidence is structurally expensive to forge (though a patient attacker who insinuates themselves into the network can eventually forge it too — the caveat #94 already flags). Production MDM already ships non-destructive, reversible merge ([Reltio unmerge](https://docs.reltio.com/en/explore/get-a-crash-course/get-ready-to-turn-your-data-into-action/learn-about-multidomain-mdm/reltio-match-merge-and-survivorship)); incremental-ER systems that maintain a provenance index of each clustering decision so they can split the closure on retraction independently reinvented assumption-stamping from the ER side.

Assumption-stamped derivations are made practical by **provenance semirings** ([Green, Karvounarakis & Tannen, PODS 2007](https://web.cs.ucdavis.edu/~green/papers/pods07.pdf)): the paper supplies the provenance polynomials and the retract-by-annihilator mechanics; the identification of a provenance polynomial with an ATMS label (a monomial as an environment) is the lane's own synthesis — sound, verified as such, but not a claim the paper itself makes. The knob that answers ATMS label explosion is: keep only as much of the label as severance needs. For zuihitsu, every derived event carries a small stamp of the revocable assumptions in force (chiefly which merges it treated as holding), and severance is a fold filter that voids stamped events and re-derives from the separated stubs.

Credence should be **subjective-logic-shaped** (belief/disbelief/uncertainty/base rate), because it separates strength-of-belief from amount-of-evidence — the distinction Bayesian credence loses ([Josang; diva-portal](https://www.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:3403/FULLTEXT02)). It supplies trust discounting (weight an attestation by teller reliability) and independent-versus-dependent fusion (refuse spurious confidence gain from recited, dependent attestations — the recitation defence again). Crucially, credence must come from **counting evidence, not from LLM-verbalised confidence**, which is systematically overconfident, saturated at coarse values, and elicitation-protocol-sensitive ([arXiv:2605.27752](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.27752); [arXiv:2604.01457](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.01457) — these are future-dated relative to the training cutoff and flagged for verification). Belief revision should be non-prioritised (credibility-limited / source-sensitive): a teller is not always right ([arXiv:2409.07119](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.07119)).

The behaviour leak (#104) is answered by the Soar/ACT-R **data/metadata wall**: identity bookkeeping is architectural metadata the agent cannot reason over, and the agent sees one resolved handle per person ([ACS 2021 ACT-R/Soar comparison](https://advancesincognitivesystems.github.io/acs2021/data/ACS-21_paper_6.pdf)). Resolution happens in the substrate before the agent ever reasons; there is nothing for it to second-guess.

### 2.4 Time and memory typology (lane `time-memory.md`)

Validity intervals belong on facts and relations: SQL:2011, TSQL2, and every interval-based temporal KG attach an interval to the edge itself, with left-open, right-open, and closed forms, and supersession by window-closing rather than deletion ([temporal KG overview](https://medium.com/self-study-notes/temporal-knowledge-graphs-5b032671e0c7); [bitemporal property graphs](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-05281-0_15)). Two supersession axes should be kept distinct: transaction-time ("I was wrong, it was never true") and valid-time ("it was true, then stopped"). A third, decision-time axis (Zep's observed-versus-recorded split) is what delayed bulk ingestion (#44) and episodic recall (#74) need ([Zep](https://www.getzep.com/ai-agents/temporal-knowledge-graph/)). Qualitative anchoring generalises `BeforeAfter` to a **tractable subset of Allen's interval algebra** with a composition table; full 13-relation reasoning is NP-hard, and the verification pass pinned the boundary — ORD-Horn is the unique maximal tractable subclass containing all basic relations (Nebel & Bürckert, JACM 1995).

The schedule/description conflation (failure class 4) is answered by iCalendar's three-component split: **VEVENT** (a descriptive occurrence — `DTSTART`), **VTODO** (a deontic task — `DUE`), and **VALARM** (a trigger — a `TRIGGER` offset relative to the occurrence plus an `ACTION`) ([RFC 5545 VTODO](https://icalendar.org/iCalendar-RFC-5545/3-6-2-to-do-component.html); [VALARM](https://icalendar.org/iCalendar-RFC-5545/3-6-6-alarm-component.html)). A VEVENT with no VALARM is a pure description that never fires. The one-line diagnosis: the wake-up scheduler arms on the descriptive axis because there is no deontic axis to arm on. Typed dates, durations, and recurrences (#103) follow the Temporal/jiff model: civil versus absolute, anchor-aware `Span` durations (killing the fixed-30-day month), and recurrence constructors that cannot encode the `BYMONTHDAY=31` pathology ([Temporal, MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/blog/javascript-temporal-is-coming/); [python-dateutil rrule](https://dateutil.readthedocs.io/en/stable/rrule.html)).

Memory is not one kind. The cognitive-architecture tradition (Tulving -> Soar/ACT-R -> CoALA) and modern LLM-agent memory work converge on a four-way typology, and zuihitsu's three "nowhere principled to live" issues are each a different kind ([CoALA, arXiv:2309.02427](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.02427); [Soar intro, arXiv:2205.03854](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.03854)): **semantic** (curated facts = today's ContentEntry), **episodic** (#74: the conversation/tool log as an automatic, read-only, provenance-marked fallback tier — [Soar episodic memory](https://soar.eecs.umich.edu/soar_manual/07_EpisodicMemory/)), **procedural** (#58: executable Luau indexed by a description embedding, Voyager's design, decayed by invocation — [Voyager, arXiv:2305.16291](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291)), and **working** (#59: a private scratchpad, promote-or-discard). The payoff against class 1: not every memory should obey the same lifecycle; forcing four kinds through one visibility predicate, one cosine-dedup, and one volatility decay is wrong for three of them.

Embedder-geometry thresholds (failure class 9) are answered two ways: replace ad-hoc `exp(-dt/tau)` decay with **ACT-R base-level activation** (recency plus frequency of access, power-law, over the log's own timestamps — embedder-invariant and replay-deterministic, [Petrov ACT-R](http://act-r.psy.cmu.edu/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2012/12/652petrovAbstract.pdf)) plus spreading activation over the relation graph; and make dedup/consolidation thresholds **distribution-relative** (percentile/F1-calibrated against the current embedder, recomputed on `EmbeddingModelChanged`) so 0.95/0.85 stop being magic constants ([cosine-drift](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/cosine-similarity-shift); [embedding-drift detection](https://www.evidentlyai.com/blog/embedding-drift-detection)) [ACT-R's transfer from human recall to agent-memory salience is by analogy, not proof; flagged].

### 2.5 The welding seam (lane `welding.md`)

NELL is the closest historical analogue — a system that ran continuously from 2010 to 2018 and accumulated over 80M beliefs — and its lesson is stark: **the ontology is the drift brake**. NELL's error suppression came not from a better extractor but from coupled semi-supervised learning under ontology constraints (mutual exclusion, type checking on relation arguments, agreement between independent views); the symbolic schema did the disposing, the extractors only proposed ([Mitchell et al., CACM 2018](https://cacm.acm.org/magazines/2018/5/227193-never-ending-learning/fulltext)). Even so, NELL needed a small periodic human touch (roughly 5 minutes per relation every 10 iterations). The verified drift figures are sharper than the folklore version: the precision of *newly-promoted* beliefs declined monotonically to ~57% by iterations 45-66 (90/71/57 across the three promotion bands), with cumulative KB precision around 74% after 66 iterations — a gradual, category-specific decline in the newly-written tail, not a cliff. Autonomy is not zero human touch; it is exception-triggered touch backed by drift detection.

**Essentially no LLM-to-KG system independently verifies the neural write against its source** — the LLM is both writer and its own only judge, which is failure class 11 reproduced across the whole field. Graphiti's LLM entity resolution degrades with graph expansion ([ATOM, arXiv:2510.22590](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.22590) — verified, exact quote found; the caveat is that ATOM's measured mechanism is context-length scaling as the graph grows, not semantic drift); GraphRAG's tracked failure mode is that the LLM invents unsupported descriptions, and the field's default remedy is more prompting, which is the prompt-sensitivity trap ([microsoft/graphrag #1543](https://github.com/microsoft/graphrag/issues/1543)). The field verifies shape and at best internal consistency, almost never faithfulness or drift. Solving it is genuinely novel work; copying Graphiti wholesale imports the blind spot.

The boundary-placement doctrine is **neural proposes, symbolic disposes, at a crisp typed interface** (DeepProbLog's neural predicate, Scallop's provenance-semiring-tagged relations, [Scallop PLDI 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.04812)), sized for an LLM by LLM-modulo: auto-regressive LLMs cannot reliably self-verify, so hard properties get a sound symbolic verifier and only soft properties may be judged by another LLM ([Kambhampati et al., ICML 2024, arXiv:2402.01817](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01817)). Provenance is computed with the conclusion, not attached post hoc.

Prompt brittleness is large, persistent, and non-transferable: FormatSpread measured accuracy spreads of dozens of points from meaning-preserving formatting changes, unfixed by scale, few-shot, or instruction tuning, and format rankings do not transfer across models ([Sclar et al., ICLR 2024, arXiv:2310.11324](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.11324) — the specific spread figures were search-sourced because the PDF did not extract, and are flagged). So the 6%-to-75% jump is the expected behaviour of a load-bearing action riding on wording, and it will reappear on every model upgrade. Move such behaviour off the prompt into **forced-choice structured elicitation** (a required schema field the turn cannot complete without filling), with the caution that structured constraints are not free — the "Constraint Tax" can suppress tool-calling or degrade reasoning in some models ([arXiv:2606.25605](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.25605) — future-dated, flagged). In-block LLM calls (#100) fit the log as record-at-call-time non-deterministic activities, exactly Temporal's determinism model ([Temporal workflow definition](https://docs.temporal.io/workflow-definition)).

### 2.6 Convergent evolution (lanes `survey-giants.md` and `survey-issue7.md`)

Across seven historical/production systems and ten current-generation personal agents, the same primitives keep being reinvented, and the same things keep being missing.

Reinvented: a **credence value riding alongside a fact** (NELL's candidate/promoted threshold, OpenCog's (strength, confidence) TruthValue, Mem0's ADD/UPDATE/DELETE/NOOP, hermes-agent's feedback-trained per-fact trust score); **never delete, only close the interval** (Graphiti's edge invalidation, porrima's bidirectional supersession edges, zuihitsu's own tombstones); **scope-as-context** (Cyc's microtheories, though for logical consistency rather than confidentiality); and **relation-vocabulary drift resolved by a closed enum plus a free-text side channel plus deterministic migration** (bound's edge-graph normalization RFC, where 78% of edges were one-off free-text relations, and LifeOS's entity-type consolidation — two independent live case studies of zuihitsu's failure classes 3 and 6, [bound](https://github.com/bound-agents/bound/blob/main/docs/design/specs/2026-04-20-edge-graph-normalization.md)).

Missing, everywhere: **no surveyed system has zuihitsu's per-fact audience/visibility posture model** — the sharpest thing zuihitsu already has over the entire current generation; **no system has a structured typed fact shape as its default** (all store prose, even the ones with typed metadata around it); and **no system stores provenance-of-evidence for a belief** (they record that a fact was updated or rated, not the evidence a judgement rested on). The graveyard lessons: Cyc's curation cost did not fall with scale (curation cost must be sub-linear in fact volume or the system dies of its own weight, even without technically failing); OpenCog's storage-and-bookkeeping layer survived 15+ years while its general reasoning layer (PLN) was abandoned and restarted [the "Fossils" characterisation is an OpenCog-community-internal judgement, flagged]; and the Zep/Mem0/LoCoMo benchmark dispute — with an independent audit finding 6.4% of the answer key wrong and the LLM judge accepting 63% of intentionally wrong answers ([bloo-mind audit](https://essays.bloo-mind.ai/posts/2026-05-20-mem-eval/) — future-dated, flagged) — is a governance lesson reinforcing zuihitsu's existing eval discipline: never let a component's success be measured by a self-reported, judge-scored composite benchmark.

## 3. The proposed ontology

The design has one keystone and eight facets that hang off it. The keystone is a single first-class assertion object. The facets are events and roles, relations, identity, belief, time, memory kinds, privacy and forgetting, and the query surface. The guiding constraint throughout is the set of fixed points: append-only log as sole truth with deterministic replay; privacy at least as strong as today; teachable-error pedagogy with a handle-shaped, simple agent surface; unbounded scale with bulk ingestion handled natively.

### 3.1 The keystone: one first-class Statement object

Today three concepts — ContentEntry, Attestation, and the scattered provenance metadata — carry the weight of "a thing was asserted". The redesign unifies them into one reified, addressable object. Call it a **Statement**. Four independent literatures (PROV bundles, nanopub pubinfo, named graphs, CKR) reinvented "make the assertion a first-class, addressable, context-scoped object" (lane `provenance-privacy.md`); the highest-leverage single move in the whole redesign is to get that one object right, because it dissolves failure classes 3, 8, and 11 simultaneously and gives forgetting and the inter-agent boundary a shared substrate.

A Statement is the Wikidata statement model crossed with TypeDB's role-typing and the neo-Davidsonian event shape (lane `fact-shape.md`). It carries:

- **A structured claim.** Either a typed `(subject, relation, object)` triple or a reference to an **Event node** with role-edges (see 3.2). This is the structure that retires "facts are sentences": `same (subject, relation, object)?` is a structural equality, not a cosine guess.
- **A prose gloss.** The original utterance, retained beside the structure, never above it (the Zep-episode / MemCube "keep both" pattern). The gloss is the safety net when the structure is wrong, the source for re-derivation, and the audit answer to failure class 11. Prose is a rendering of the structured fact, subordinate to it, not the source of truth.
- **Provenance qualifiers**, one level deep only (the Wikidata discipline against qualifier-scope ambiguity): who told it (`told_by`), where (`told_in`), and the observed-versus-recorded decision-time pair (see 3.6).
- **Bitemporal timestamps plus a valid-time interval** (see 3.6): `asserted_at`/`recorded_at` on the transaction axis, `observed_at` on the decision axis, and a `[valid_from, valid_to)` interval (left- or right-open) on the world axis.
- **A credence**, a subjective-logic opinion derived from evidence structure, never from a verbalised number (see 3.5).
- **A transmission principle** — the generalisation of the visibility posture, a predicate over the present set and the log history (see 3.8).
- **A derivation record with an assumption stamp** — for derived Statements (a distillation, a consolidation, a link inference), the PROV-shaped record of premises, activity, agent, plan/template, plus the small set of revocable assumptions in force at production time (see 3.5, 3.8, and the welding architecture in 4).

The Statement is the atomic unit the whole system reads and writes. Attestation is not a separate concept; a Statement with multiple `told_by` tellers is "a fact a set of tellers stand behind", and each teller's endorsement carries its own transmission principle and its own retraction authority (the nanopub who-may-retract move). The audience-widening invariant, the hidden-endorsement zero-residue semantics, and per-attester retraction with last-attestation death all survive, expressed now as properties of one object rather than of a ContentEntry-plus-Attestation pair.

One honest cost, stated up front: every fact is now a small graph, not a row. More nodes, more edges, more materialisation cost, more query surface. The n-ary-embedding literature's consolation is that once a graph is genuinely n-ary the on-disk reification syntax barely matters — spend the design budget on the event/role model, not on bikeshedding the SQLite encoding ([arXiv:2503.21804](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21804) — read from the abstract only; the "encoding matters less once genuinely n-ary" claim is flagged for verification against the full text).

### 3.2 Events and roles: the fix for one-event-many-copies

A multi-participant happening is **one Event node with role-edges**, not per-subject prose copies. "Alice and Bob shipped feature X on Tuesday" is one `event/ship` with two `Agent` edges (or `Agent` and `Co-agent`), one `Theme` (feature X), and one `Time` (Tuesday). Each role-edge and each attribute is an independently addressable, independently attributable, independently visible Statement, which composes cleanly with the per-teller posture model. This is failure class 2's principled fix, well-precedented across AMR, the W3C n-ary Pattern 2, and TypeDB's `deal(target, buyer, target-advisor, buyer-advisor)` (lane `fact-shape.md`).

The load-bearing constraint is that **the role set stays small and universal**. PropBank's own numbered roles above ARG0/ARG1 are documented as inconsistent, and even expert annotators disagree on the tail; FrameNet's ~1,200 frame-specific inventories are expressive but unteachable to a live single-utterance writer ([Stanford SLP3 SRL slides](https://web.stanford.edu/~jurafsky/slp3/slides/22_SRL.pdf)). So zuihitsu takes a small closed universal role set — `agent`, `patient`/`theme`, `instrument`, `time`, `place`, `recipient`, and a small handful more — and **everything else is an attribute on the Event, not another role**. When the writer is unsure which role fits, the gloss preserves the surface form and a proto-role-style hedge is available. This keeps the writer's burden teachable: the evidence is that schema-guided writing lowers the LLM's error rate, but role assignment above agent/patient is genuinely hard, so the universal set must be kept small.

The implementation-event that was recorded three times in one session (survey evidence, seqs 436-440 and 590-595) becomes one Event: one `agent` role on the person who built the bot, one `instrument` or `source` role on the architecture that was shared, one `time` (two days), with the abstracting topic linked by a `part_of`/`about` edge rather than re-phrased. The flush-burst duplication disappears because there is nothing to re-phrase — the second and third mentions resolve to the existing Event node (a structural NOOP, see 4).

### 3.3 Relations: attribute-bearing, interval-scoped, schema-evolving

A relation is promoted from a bare edge to a first-class Statement carrying attributes (Wikidata statement / property-graph edge-properties / W3C n-ary Pattern 1). It gains:

- **A validity interval** so "worked at X from 2019 to 2021" is a relation, not prose — supersession by window-closing, never deletion (the Graphiti/Zep bitemporal pattern, corroborated as convergent across lanes `fact-shape.md`, `time-memory.md`, and `survey-giants.md`).
- **Provenance and credence**, as for any Statement.
- **Declared domain and range (role types)** on the relation *definition*, so a reversed or mis-typed edge is caught as a teachable error at write time. Reversed-relationship errors are a named, common LLM extraction failure mode, and a declared domain/range is the highest-value structural guard against them ([anchor-constrained grounded KG extraction, MDPI Computers 15(3):178](https://www.mdpi.com/2073-431X/15/3/178)).

Relation vocabulary evolves by **deprecate-and-alias**, event-sourced. A relation definition carries a lifecycle (`active` / `deprecated` / `aliased_to: <canonical>`). A mis-coined relation is deprecated and aliased; reads resolve aliases transitively; the "coined four ways" drift collapses to four aliases of one canonical relation at read time. This directly retires failure class 6 / #42, and it is not a novel bet: bound's edge-graph normalization RFC and LifeOS's entity-type consolidation are two independent production instances of exactly this fix shape (closed enum, single source of truth, free-text `context` side channel, deterministic startup migration), and both needed a *migratable* enum, not an immutable one (lane `survey-issue7.md`). The free-text `context` column bound added — to carry the bespoke phrasing that used to be smuggled into the relation field — is worth adopting verbatim: it is the escape hatch that keeps the closed enum honest.

Coining a relation requires declaring inverse, cardinality, **and** domain/range (the registry has the first two today). This is the Wikidata "specify domain and range before creation" discipline and the TypeDB role-filler-type enforcement, and it is what makes the reversed-edge guard possible. The seed ontology stays a minimum-viable structural set (identity, participation, composition, placement, origin, operatorship, acquaintance); social and environmental semantics remain the agent's to coin at runtime, now against a schema that can catch misuse and evolve.

### 3.4 Identity: revocable composite identity behind a wall

Drop union-find `class_id` as *the* identity structure. Keep stubs-forever plus `same_as`-as-assumption, but make each merge a first-class, credence-bearing **assumption** (`merge#k`) rather than a boolean transitive union. The literature is unanimous that hard `owl:sameAs`-style equivalence is the wrong primitive, and transitivity is the amplifier that turns one wrong merge into a corrupted class (lane `identity-belief.md`). The class becomes an assumption-conditioned *view* folded from the log, so a wrong merge is a retraction, not a fossil.

Three mechanisms make this work together:

1. **Graded, contextual, revocable merges.** A merge carries a subjective-logic opinion, strengthens with corroboration, and crumbles on divergence. The Gamma/Lambda split gives the formal licence to separate "same for recall" (indiscernibility, Gamma) from "same for disclosure" (propagation, Lambda): recall can unify a person's history while disclosure stays gated ([sameAs survey](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1907.10528)). Collective ER on the graph's native relational structure (participation, acquaintance, placement) is the passive signal — two stubs independently linked to the same third parties by *different* tellers is expensive to forge, unlike recited attribute overlap (Bhattacharya & Getoor). It is currently unused and is the strongest available signal; it raises attacker cost without closing the gap (the patient-attacker caveat stands).

2. **Assumption-stamped derivations, severance as a fold filter.** Every derived event carries a small stamp of the revocable assumptions in force — chiefly which merges it treated as holding. Recording a `MergeSevered` event does not mutate history; on replay, events stamped with the retracted `merge#k` are voided. The fold filter is precisely and only that deterministic voiding — "the world as if never merged" is `fold(events, drop everything stamped merge#k)`, the sameAs.cc "split the transitive closure" operation, computed rather than forensically reconstructed, with no model call anywhere in replay. *Re-derivation* from the now-separated stubs is a distinct, subsequent record-time maintenance pass (it drives the model and embedder, and its outputs land as new events), so the determinism fixed point is untouched. The stamp stays minimal (only revocable assumptions, usually zero or one merge per derivation), which is the provenance-semiring answer to ATMS label explosion.

3. **The data/metadata wall (the #104 fix).** All of this lives *below the substrate wall*. The agent's operational view is "this is Priya, one handle"; the substrate's representational view is "class of stubs {chat, forum} under revocable assumption `merge#k`". The agent never chooses between `person/priya`, `person/priya@chat`, and `person/priya@forum` — the substrate resolves the current speaker to a canonical handle and hands it to the agent, which writes to what it is given (killing #104 cluster 1, the bare-handle minting) and has nothing to second-guess (killing #104 cluster 2, the handle-equality testing). This is the Soar/ACT-R architectural-metadata wall applied to identity, and it is the single most important lesson for keeping the operational surface simple: **the substrate does all resolution; the agent-facing surface is one resolved handle per person** (the #104 lesson, lane `identity-belief.md`).

Reversibility is tiered (see 6): revocable effects (unified recall, composite briefs, distilled descriptions) ride the tentative merge and are safe to be imperfect because severance genuinely undoes them; the irrevocable effect (a confidence spoken across the identity boundary to a real person) stays gated on the hard bar of challenge-response (#93), because TMS gives mechanical severance of internal state and is silent on external disclosure.

### 3.5 Belief: credence from evidence, never from the model's mouth

Every Statement carries a **subjective-logic opinion** (belief, disbelief, uncertainty, base rate) as its credence datatype, for both facts and merges, because it separates strength-of-belief from amount-of-evidence — precisely what an aggregating agent most needs and what Bayesian credence conflates (lane `identity-belief.md`). The opinion is derived from **evidence counting** — the number and independence of attestations and the tellers' track records, via the opinion-to-evidence-count (Beta/Dirichlet) mapping — **not** from the LLM verbalising a probability, because verbalised confidence is overconfident, saturates to coarse values, and shifts with the elicitation protocol. Where the model must supply a judgement, it is quantised to a coarse ordinal (suspected / likely / confirmed), matching the granularity the model can actually calibrate.

Two subjective-logic operators do real work. **Trust discounting** weights an attestation by teller reliability (belief mass bleeds into uncertainty for a partially-reliable teller) — this is credibility-limited / source-sensitive belief revision made arithmetic. **Independent-versus-dependent fusion** is the recitation defence at the belief layer: two attestations that are actually one recited fact are fused as dependent (no confidence gain), not independent (spurious gain). Belief revision is non-prioritised: a low-credibility attestation does not overturn a high-credibility one, replacing the current episodic prose-note arbitration and the persist-as-a-flag handling of contradictions.

The surface stays ordinal and provenance-anchored — "three people told me this, one usually unreliable" beats "confidence 0.72" — consistent with the handle-shaped-simple fixed point: credence lives in the substrate as an opinion tuple and surfaces as a coarse, explainable posture with its evidence attached.

**Where the lanes disagree, recorded honestly.** The identity-belief lane argues hard for subjective logic as the credence datatype. The convergent-evolution survey (lane `survey-giants.md`) counsels that three concretely different tested shapes exist — a single scalar strength, OpenCog's (strength, confidence) pair, and Mem0's four-way categorical decision — and that the redesign should choose deliberately rather than treat "add a credence model" as settled. The two are reconcilable but the choice is real: subjective logic is the richest and the best-motivated for the recitation defence (its dependent-fusion distinction is the load-bearing feature), but it is also the heaviest, and its fusion operators have named critics ("Can we trust subjective logic for information fusion?", which argues some operators are not associative). The lane's own hedge is the right resolution: **adopt subjective logic's representation and its discounting operator, and be conservative about claiming any one fusion operator is principled** — validate a specific fusion operator against those objections before relying on it. The verification pass sharpened this further: the same critique also attacks the link between opinions and Beta probability density functions, which is exactly the opinion-to-evidence-count mapping the credence derivation leans on — so the conservatism extends to that mapping, not only to fusion. The recitation defence survives this conservatism, because its load-bearing part is dependence *detection* (a provenance determination, operator-free) plus a conservative no-gain rule for dependent evidence — in the degenerate case an idempotent "no update", which is trivially sound — rather than any contested fusion arithmetic. If the full SL machinery proves too heavy for a first version, hermes-agent's feedback-trained per-fact trust score is the existence proof that a lighter credence layer sits on an ordinary fact store (lane `survey-issue7.md`) — an 80% fallback, explicitly a fallback.

### 3.6 Time: the schedule/description split and typed values

Three fields that today are one occurrence field are separated, on the iCalendar model:

1. **Descriptive occurrence** (`occurred_at`, kept): the valid-time placement of the referent. A fact *describing* another bot's cron job legitimately has `Recurring(FREQ=DAILY)` here — that is true of the world. This is the VEVENT `DTSTART` axis.
2. **Deontic due** (new): present *only* when the agent itself is meant to act — a first-class **Task** object (VTODO `DUE`). A description has none.
3. **Trigger** (new): a first-class **Trigger** object (VALARM), a fire-time expressed relative to the deontic due plus an explicit action. The wake-up scheduler arms on the Trigger, never on the bare descriptive occurrence.

The one-line resolution of failure class 4: the scheduler currently arms on the descriptive axis because there is no deontic axis to arm on. Introduce a Task/Trigger layer and a dated *description* — the other bot's cron, the namesake's 1789 birth year — can never fire, because only a Task with a Trigger fires, and a Task is something the agent authored for itself, never something it merely recorded about the world. This also retires `docs/time.md`'s admitted `#due` overload.

**A disagreement the survey surfaced, kept visible.** The current-generation survey (lane `survey-issue7.md`) found that failure class 4 is *not* corroborated as a general failure by any surveyed peer — none of them layer a calendar-as-a-view over the same per-fact temporal field that describes when something happened, because none has a fact-level temporal field of that richness at all. So the schedule/description conflation may be relatively specific to zuihitsu's design. This does not weaken the fix — iCalendar is mature prior art from the calendaring domain, and the split is clean — but it does mean the redesign should not expect external corroboration that the *problem* is universal, only that the *solution shape* (separate occurrence, task, and trigger) is well-established. Recorded so the fix is not oversold.

Dates, durations, and recurrences become first-class typed values (#103), backed by `jiff::civil::Date` / `jiff::Span` end to end, strings only at the input boundary. Civil-versus-absolute is honoured (Day civil versus Instant absolute, already present); durations become anchor-aware `Span`s that resolve against the query's `now`, killing the fixed-30-day month; and recurrence is exposed through *constructors* that cannot encode the `BYMONTHDAY=31` trap rather than as raw RRULE strings. The two affordances #103 requires are preserved: a typed value stands in directly as an `occurred_at`, and stringifies to its ISO form under interpolation. A tractable subset of Allen's interval algebra (before/after/during/overlaps/meets with a composition table) generalises `BeforeAfter` so the temporal graph can *infer* orderings (bound to a tractable subclass — ORD-Horn is the verified maximal one; the pointisable subalgebra may already suffice for the anchors the agent writes).

### 3.7 Memory typology: four kinds with distinct lifecycles

Memory *kind* becomes a first-class type distinction, because each kind has different lifecycle, authority, retrieval, and decay rules — that is the whole point of the typology, not mere labelling.

- **Semantic** (today's ContentEntry, now a Statement): durable, credence-bearing, visibility-gated, distilled into descriptions. Authority: Platform (turns) plus Agent (maintenance). This is the curated-fact kind.
- **Episodic** (#74): the conversation and tool-result log exposed as an *automatic, read-only, provenance-marked fallback retrieval tier*. It is raw experience — "what was said", not "what is true" — so it is never edited, never distilled into descriptions, aggressively recency-decayed in retrieval, and surfaced only when semantic search misses, with staleness markers. This matches Soar's design where episodic memory is architectural and automatic while semantic is deliberate. Critically, an episode must never be laundered into asserted truth: consolidating a raw episode as if it were a curated fact turns "someone said X" into "X is true", which is a distinct and dangerous error the type distinction prevents.
- **Procedural** (#58): executable Luau indexed by a natural-language description embedding (Voyager's exact design), decayed by *invocation* recency and frequency (ACT-R base-level activation) rather than calendar age — a correct-but-unused procedure is not stale the way a fact is. Retrieved on demand to bound prompt cost; produced by the reflection pass (#20); invoked in the same frozen sandbox under the same step and timeout budget. Soar's chunking-on-impasse suggests a further refinement: procedural memory could be created *automatically* from a costly or repeated deliberation (a turn that required unusually deep multi-step Luau reasoning is zuihitsu's impasse analogue) rather than only from the agent deciding to save a function (lane `survey-giants.md`).
- **Working / scratchpad** (#59): persistent-but-transient, private to the agent, *outside the visibility model entirely* (it is the agent's own head). The staging area: reflection promotes items to semantic memory or discards them.

**A weakest-confidence recommendation, flagged as such by its lane.** How the working scratchpad is stored is a genuine open call (lane `time-memory.md`). The event log is the source of truth, but scratchpad churn is exactly the transient state that should not bloat replay. The lane leans toward keeping it in the log as a *compactable* channel — a `ScratchpadWritten`/`ScratchpadCleared` pair whose net effect after consolidation is a single promote-or-discard, analogous to how superseded blocks are folded — preserving "system is a pure function of the log" while keeping the working set bounded. But it flags this as its weakest recommendation, trading replay-purity against log size, and a side-table is a defensible alternative. This is left as an explicit open question in section 8.

Bulk ingestion (#44) is handled natively by this typology: a long document lands as **semantic Statement clusters** (schema-guided extraction, sections and claims becoming Events and Statements linked by `part_of`/`summarizes` role-edges into a navigable hypergraph — the W3C n-ary composition pattern) plus an **episodic source layer** retaining each source span as the gloss, so a bulk-ingested claim is always traceable to its source text. The decision-time `observed_at`/`recorded_at` split is what makes delayed ingestion coherent — a document authored in 2019 and ingested in 2026 records both.

### 3.8 Privacy, provenance, and forgetting

Keep and strengthen the posture model. The posture enum becomes the **default library** of a small, registered, closed vocabulary of **transmission principles**, each compiling to a deterministic predicate over (present set, log history): `in_confidence`, `attributed`, `reciprocal`, `with_consent(event)`, `purpose(reminder)`. This is contextual integrity's transmission principle promoted to first-class data (lane `provenance-privacy.md`). It buys three things the four-value enum cannot: confidences that travel across the inter-agent boundary (a local enum cannot cross; a principle-as-data can); the subject-guard as a *derived* negative norm ("never to recipients in set S", of which "never to the subject" is one instance); and a principled privacy story for calendar and reminder flows (`purpose(reminder)`), which touches failure class 4's intent-leak.

Audiences are **first-class contexts in the data model but a deterministic predicate in the evaluator**. Each Statement is scoped to a context expressed as a predicate over present participants (generalising `Exclude(set)`), borrowing CKR's per-context truth and justifiable-exceptions framing (the subject-guard is a justifiable exception) and named-graphs' quotation-versus-assertion split (`Attributed` = quoted, `Public` = asserted). But CKR's knowledge-propagation engine is *not* imported — propagation is the opposite of compartmentalisation — and `visible()` stays a cheap, fail-closed, deterministic predicate, not a DL cross-context query. The powerset-of-audiences blowup is real; predicates-over-present-set is the only tractable form (lane `provenance-privacy.md`, which flags that no CKR work handles a combinatorial/dynamic context population well — a genuine gap, not the lane's ignorance).

The zero-residue fixed point is treated as **non-interference**: an uncleared surface must be observationally equivalent to one where the hidden fact was never known. Because distillation is a *derived* flow and the negative norm binds derived flows, the `Public`-only distillation rule (the write-time compartmentalisation guarantee) is held as a non-interference invariant, not a convention — `Public` distillation is declassification, a transmission principle that permits an otherwise-forbidden aggregation. c6k's "nobody synthesizes the character to itself" rule suggests an explicit parallel invariant worth stating: a description is a synthesis of *others'* stated facts, never a synthesis of the agent's own private reasoning about a memory (lane `survey-issue7.md`).

The derivation record adopts the **PROV shape** (activity `wasGeneratedBy` the conclusion, `used` the premises, `wasAssociatedWith` the agent via a `qualifiedAssociation` whose plan is the template and whose agent names the model), upgrading `produced_by` from "records model and template" to "records model, template, premises, agent, time, **and evidence and criteria**" — closing the specific failure-class-11 gap. On top of PROV sits the defeasible **assumption/justification layer** (ATMS via provenance semirings) that PROV deliberately lacks, so a conclusion's truth is recorded as *contingent* on its assumptions, and retracting an assumption propagates.

Forgetting distinguishes two appended, signed operations:

- **Retract**: append a tombstone; content survives for audit; reversible. The existing supersession/retraction discipline.
- **Forget**: crypto-shred the payload (per-subject/per-atom keys) or delete a forgettable payload where the law demands true deletion (GDPR treats ciphertext as still personal data), keeping the hash-chained *envelope* so the log stays tamper-evident and the fact of forgetting is itself auditable without revealing content. Replay is deterministic over the post-forget log. **Erasure propagates through the derivation graph**: shredding a premise retracts its support and the truth-maintenance layer recomputes what still holds, rather than leaving conclusions dangling on a hole. Forgetting without a defeasible derivation layer produces incoherent replay — this is the second independent argument (with identity severance) for building the ATMS layer.

Forgetting and retraction authority is a **lattice over {teller, subject, operator}**: the teller can tombstone; the subject and operator can force erasure the teller alone could only tombstone — the nanopub who-may-retract question, answered for the subject-differs-from-author case that nanopubs leave open. Inter-agent claims enter as **quotations, not assertions** (`told_by = Agent` implies `Attributed` by construction, never distilled until independently corroborated), carrying a content-addressed reference to the source agent's attestation (so its retraction propagates), the travelling transmission principle (so cross-agent confidence is honoured), and assumption stamps (so a downstream agent can see and defease what a claim rests on).

### 3.9 The query surface: structural reads, still handle-shaped

Structural questions get structural answers. Beside search, the agent surface gains first-class **structural reads** — who, when, and how-do-you-know — that read the graph directly: "who did X build?" traverses `agent` role-edges rather than searching prose; "when did she work at Y?" reads the relation's validity interval; "how do you know?" reads the derivation record and evidence. These stay handle-shaped and simple — the agent addresses people and memories by handle and asks structured questions, never speaking ontology-language. The richness is entirely in the substrate; the surface exposes a small vocabulary of typed reads and the same teachable-error pedagogy. This is the query-side payoff of moving structure out of prose: the reads that today can only be answered by search-plus-prose become deterministic graph queries.

## 4. The welding architecture

This is the core. The fact model of section 3 is necessary but not sufficient: a typed store still fails if the neural writer fills it unverifiably. The welding architecture specifies the seam — what crosses it, what disposes of what crosses, how provenance is computed, how in-block neural calls fit the deterministic log, and how drift is managed over a lifetime.

### 4.1 A typed seam: the writer proposes structure, not prose

The neural agent sits on the **proposal** side of a typed seam. Its writes cross as *typed structured proposals* — a neo-Davidsonian Event with typed roles, a typed temporal interval, a transmission principle, a relation instance against a typed schema — never as an un-typed prose sentence. This is the structural repair for failure class 1: if the seam is typed, no downstream mechanism re-parses prose, because the structure was captured at write time. The prose gloss still exists as a human-readable rendering, derived-from and subordinate-to the structure.

Adopt the DeepProbLog/Scallop/LLM-modulo doctrine wholesale *as doctrine* (not as machinery — zuihitsu is not embedding a Datalog differentiator): **the neural half never gets to be the final authority on a structural question**. DeepProbLog's neural predicate has a fixed arity and type; the neural output cannot smuggle unstructured prose across the seam. zuihitsu's current seam is a prose sentence — the maximally un-typed interface, which is why every downstream mechanism must re-parse it. Typing the seam inverts that.

### 4.2 Neural proposes, symbolic disposes: a bank of critics

Per LLM-modulo's core finding that auto-regressive LLMs cannot reliably self-verify, replace "model judgement polices model-written structure" (an LLM grading itself, which the current maintenance passes do) with a bank of critics that run at write time and can *reject* a write:

- **Hard critics — sound, symbolic, gating.** Type and domain/range checks on relation arguments; mutual-exclusion constraints (NELL's drift brake — a thing cannot be both a city and a person); audience-visibility invariants (no attestation wider than founding posture, now a write-time critic rather than a hoped-for property); `same_as`-authority checks; temporal-interval well-formedness (`valid_from <= valid_to`); and a **duplicate-window NOOP** check that resolves a re-mention to an existing Event/Statement rather than appending a copy. A write violating a hard critic is rejected with a *teachable error* — the pedagogy zuihitsu already uses, now backed by a sound check rather than a hope. This is the direct fix for failure class 11: the writer is no longer unverified, because every structural write must pass sound critics that check well-formedness against the ontology, not merely authority and visibility.
- **Soft critics — LLM-based, sampled, non-gating.** "Is this a good summary?", "are these two descriptions the same claim?" These are genuinely soft judgements where being occasionally wrong is tolerable; they are sampled-and-voted (self-consistency), never single-shot, and never gate a hard property.

The decision rule for which behaviours move off the prompt is **failure mode**: any behaviour whose failure is a *silent correctness or safety failure* moves into harness-enforced structure — fact capture, visibility-posture setting, speaker resolution, temporal placement, the schedule-versus-description distinction, the decision to fire a wake-up. Each becomes a **forced-choice structured elicitation**: a native function with a required schema the turn cannot complete without filling, so the behaviour is a structural requirement rather than a scaffold sentence the model may or may not honour. The verification pass tempers the causal story, and the tempered version is what the design commits to: a required field eliminates the *omission* variance (the 6%-versus-75% "did it capture at all" swing collapses, because capture is no longer a function of wording), but it relocates variance into the field's *content*, and it adds a junk-fill failure mode — the model must put something in the slot, possibly wrong or low-confidence. So forced-choice elicitation is paired with the hard critics (which reject malformed content), an explicit "nothing to record" option (so the escape from the requirement is a recorded decision, not junk), and content-level evaluation (see section 5). What stays prompt-borne is genuinely generative or stylistic behaviour — how a description is phrased, conversational style, which valid framing the agent chooses. The scaffold teaches *principles and taste* (which tolerate wording drift); the harness enforces *load-bearing steps* (which do not).

**A cost that must be owned, not waved away.** Structured constraints are not free: the "Constraint Tax" study finds that imposing structured-output constraints can suppress tool-calling or degrade reasoning in some open-weight models (lane `welding.md`, [arXiv:2606.25605](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.25605) — future-dated, flagged). So the move is not "schema-constrain everything" — it is "schema-constrain the load-bearing structural writes, and leave the model free-form for the generative and deliberative parts", with an eval to confirm the constraint did not cost more than it bought. This is a real tension with the fact model's ambition to structure everything, and the eval harness (section 5) is where it is adjudicated per behaviour.

### 4.3 Provenance computed with the conclusion

Per Scallop's lesson, provenance is a structural artefact of derivation, computed *with* the conclusion, not attached as a post-hoc note. Every structural write records the evidence (the utterances it derives from), the criterion applied, and the assumption stamp (see 3.4) — this is what makes the drift audits (see 4.5) and belief revision (see 3.5, the ATMS direction) possible, and it closes the exact failure-class-11 gap that judgement provenance today records model and template but not evidence or criteria. NELL's coupling insight applies here as the strongest under-used idea in the whole survey: **require agreement between two independent signals before a structural fact is promoted to settled status** — embedding geometry and model judgement (already used together in the maintenance passes, but not as a promotion gate), or two independent tellers. Coupling is what keeps per-channel error from compounding silently; thresholded promotion is what keeps tentative beliefs from contaminating the surface the rest of the system treats as ground truth. The candidate/promoted boundary (NELL, Fellegi-Sunter's review band, subjective logic's coarse ordinals) should be explicit and inspectable — a credence and a threshold, not a vibe.

### 4.4 In-block LLM calls (#100): record-at-call-time, replay-from-log

The in-block `llm` call is the welding seam made explicit and reified — it lets the agent invoke neural judgement inside a symbolic transaction. It is built as a **record-at-call-time non-deterministic activity**, exactly Temporal's determinism model, which independently validates zuihitsu's existing rule (lane `welding.md`). The community formulation is blunt and on point: "you cannot replay an LLM call and pretend it is the same event; the output must be recorded the first time and reused during recovery" ([Koshy](https://nittikkin.medium.com/agent-workflows-are-rediscovering-durable-execution-be110661ed8c)). Concretely:

- On first (record-time) execution, the call emits a `ModelCalled`-style event carrying the prompt and response into the log before returning to the block. On replay, the same call site consumes the recorded response — no model call, deterministic continuation. This is the identical treatment `ModelCalled` already gets in the turn loop.
- The external-call latch (making a post-response block timeout a terminal error) protects the *first live* execution against a re-drive that would re-spend tokens and branch on a different answer; the recorded response protects *replay*. The two mechanisms cover the two distinct hazards (live re-drive versus log replay); #100's own no-retry-latch instinct is right, but the log makes it almost moot for replay.
- Supersession: a block mid-`llm`-call is cooperatively cancellable at the model-stream boundary, exactly as a mid-model-stream turn already is, and the recorded-partial discipline (`ModelCallAborted`) extends to the block-level call.

The design decision #100 forces: expose the in-block call **primarily as native, schema-constrained extraction/judgement functions** — `llm.extract_event`, `llm.classify_visibility`, `llm.resolve_speaker`, `llm.yes_no` — which *are* the forced-choice structured elicitation of 4.2, so #100 becomes the delivery vehicle for moving load-bearing behaviour off the prompt. A freeform `llm.complete` is treated as a *deliberative, non-committing* escape hatch only, because a freeform call with transaction-commit power re-introduces the unverified neural writer. Every `llm`-derived write still passes the hard critics of 4.2.

### 4.5 Drift management: canaries, audits, recalibration, activation

NELL is the standing proof that an autonomous accumulator drifts unless something outside the neural loop watches it, and the maintenance passes that re-derive structure from the agent's own prose are structurally a self-training loop exposed to exactly this. Four mechanisms, none of which is a better prompt:

- **The ontology is the drift brake** (NELL's lesson, 4.2). Typed, mutually-exclusive, constraint-checked structure is what a candidate write can *violate* — the thing "facts are sentences" makes impossible today. This is the primary defence; the rest are detection.
- **Canary facts.** Seed known-true, known-false, and known-private facts at genesis and re-probe them periodically over a long simulated lifetime, asserting the true ones survive, the false ones stay rejected, and the private ones never leak. A flip is a drift alarm. Drift is a longitudinal property a single-turn scenario structurally cannot see. The verified limits: canaries see only *seeded* regions, and re-derivation audits test stability, not absolute correctness — so both are weakest against exactly the drift shape NELL actually suffered, a gradual monotonic precision decline in the *newly-written* tail, and both are blind to internally-consistent poisoning. Canaries are necessary, not sufficient; the two-graph divergence monitor below and the agreement-before-promotion coupling of 4.3 are the defences aimed at the unseeded tail.
- **Periodic re-derivation audits.** Run the maintenance passes repeatedly over a growing log and assert the derived structure is stable — that dedup/consolidation does not oscillate, merge-and-unmerge, or accumulate contradictions. Re-run the audit after `EmbeddingModelChanged` and assert the derived structure did not silently shift — the specific probe for failure class 9. The strongest published drift-monitoring design builds two graphs (a deterministic rule-built baseline and the LLM-generated one), treats both as noisy sensors, and flags persistent divergence ([arXiv:2509.03857](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.03857) — future-dated, flagged); for zuihitsu the deterministic baseline is the *structured* ontology and the neural-written prose is the noisy sensor.
- **Distribution-relative thresholds and ACT-R activation.** Dedup/consolidation thresholds are calibrated from the current embedder's own similarity distribution (percentile/F1-fit) and recomputed on `EmbeddingModelChanged`, turning silent invalidation into automatic recalibration. Retrieval and decay use ACT-R base-level activation (recency plus frequency of access over the log's own timestamps) plus spreading activation over the relation graph — both embedder-invariant and replay-deterministic, a strong fit for the append-only fixed point. Cosine similarity stays as *one* term, not the sole ranking axis. porrima's two-band policy (auto-merge above a high threshold, LLM-judged in a narrow band below it) is a more principled shape than a single cutoff and is worth adopting, paired with per-space calibration rather than a hardcoded constant (lane `survey-issue7.md`).

NELL's other lesson governs the whole autonomy story: a small operator exception queue replaces continuous curation. The zero-administration endgame (#15/#20) is exception-triggered human touch backed by drift detection, not *no* human touch — and Cyc's economics warn that any mechanism whose marginal cost per fact does not fall as the log grows imports Cyc's slow death (section 8).

## 5. Evaluation design

The good news the welding lane establishes: zuihitsu's existing harness generalises cleanly to ontology-level properties, because the event log *is* the ground truth and a structural oracle can read it directly. A behavioural oracle ("the agent kept the confidence") and an ontology oracle ("no attestation is wider than its founding posture", "every `same_as` edge has an operator author", "no Statement references a retracted memory") are the same kind of object — a pure function of the event log. The `rejudge` replay mode already lets an oracle change be reclassified over recorded runs without re-running the model, which is exactly what iterating ontology-level oracles cheaply wants.

The probes, mapped to the properties the welding must have:

- **Behavioural robustness (failure class 10).** Capture-rate probes with paraphrase batteries: run the same scenario across semantically-equivalent phrasings and measure the spread (FormatSpread's methodology). The spread must be measured over the captured *field content's correctness*, not over capture presence — a required field pins presence near-uniform by construction, so a presence-only metric gives false comfort while junk-fill variance hides in the values. A behaviour correctly moved from prompt to structure shows near-zero content-correctness spread; a wide spread flags that it is still effectively prompt-borne. This turns failure class 10 from an anecdote into a continuously-monitored metric, run as the model-upgrade regression gate — because brittleness does not transfer across model generations, this is the only durable defence.
- **Extraction fidelity (failure classes 1, 11).** Gold-structure scenarios whose utterances have a known-correct structural extraction (the Event with its roles, the typed interval, the posture), scored precision/recall against the gold structure. Plus faithfulness oracles: every structural write must be entailed by some utterance in the scenario transcript — a write with no supporting utterance is a hallucinated write, the failure-class-11 signature, and fails gating. This directly targets the GraphRAG "LLM invents unsupported structure" failure.
- **Drift over a lifetime (failure classes 9, 11).** Longitudinal scenarios spanning a simulated lifetime with periodic canary re-probes and maintenance-pass audits, as a first-class scenario shape. A canary flip or an audit oscillation is a drift alarm. Re-derivation stability after `EmbeddingModelChanged` is the specific class-9 probe.
- **Scalability (#44).** Bulk-ingestion fidelity at volume: ingest a long document, then assert the resulting cluster is navigable and extraction precision did not fall relative to a short-document baseline (Graphiti's LLM resolution degrades with graph expansion — drift-with-size is the specific scalability failure the welding must not have).

The eval discipline the convergent survey underwrites (lanes `survey-giants.md` and `survey-issue7.md`): the Zep/Mem0/LoCoMo benchmark chaos — two funded companies disputing the same benchmark while an independent audit finds the answer key and judge both unreliable — is an outside argument *for* zuihitsu's existing CONTRIBUTING.md discipline (structural checks for exact properties, judge reserved for genuine language judgement, gating bars with an explicit `min_rate`), not a reason to imitate anyone's PR numbers. Never let a redesigned component's success be measured by a self-reported, judge-scored composite benchmark with no independently-agreed protocol.

When the agent's behaviour changes — a prompt, a tool, a critic, a planning step — write an eval that captures the behaviour so a later change can be assessed against it. The new scenario is the regression test for the edit; if a future tweak regresses it, the run goes red. This is the existing discipline, and the redesign does not relax it; it extends it with longitudinal and extraction-fidelity scenario shapes.

## 6. Autonomy and reversibility

The organising principle (#94, endorsed and generalised): **tiered reversibility applied to every derived conclusion**. Split effects by whether severance can genuinely undo them.

- **Revocable effects.** Unified recall, shared inference, composite briefs, distilled descriptions, consolidations, link inferences — everything internal to the substrate. These ride tentative, credence-bearing assumptions (merges, and derivations stamped with those assumptions) and are *safe to be imperfect because severance genuinely undoes them*: an assumption retraction voids the stamped derivations and re-folds the log. The Gamma/Lambda split licenses this for identity: "same for recall" (Gamma) rides the revocable tier without propagating "same for disclosure" (Lambda). Because event sourcing makes "the world as if never merged" computable and the assumption stamp makes it *findable*, imperfection here is a bounded cost, not a fossil.
- **The genuinely irreversible set.** Three things cannot be re-folded, and TMS is silent on all three because they are *external* effects: **outbound disclosure** (a confidence spoken across an identity or audience boundary to a real person — you can void a derived brief entry, but you cannot un-say it); **external side effects** (a message posted, an email sent, an action taken in the world); and **Forget** (crypto-shredding is irreversible by design). These stay gated on the hard bar: a completed challenge-response (#93) or evidence past a far stricter threshold for the identity-boundary disclosure case, and explicit operator or subject authority for Forget. The reversibility tiering is not decoration — it is the direct consequence of TMS giving mechanical severance of internal state and nothing for external effects.

The exception classes that reach the operator, with the expectation NELL sets that this is a *small* queue, not continuous curation:

- **Identity-boundary disclosure** that the substrate cannot clear by relational evidence alone — routed to challenge-response, which reaches the operator only when the active gate cannot resolve it. Expected rate: rare, bounded by genuinely ambiguous cross-platform encounters.
- **Forget requests** from a subject or teller where authority must be confirmed — the lattice forces this to a human when the requester is not the operator. Expected rate: rare, event-driven.
- **Drift alarms** — a flipped canary, an oscillating audit, a post-`EmbeddingModelChanged` structural shift. Expected rate: episodic, and the *whole point* is that it is detection-triggered rather than a standing curation duty (NELL's per-relation periodic touch, sized down to exception-triggered).
- **Hard-critic rejections the agent cannot resolve** — a persistent teachable-error loop that suggests a schema gap (a relation wanting deprecate-and-alias, a missing role) rather than an agent mistake. Expected rate: falls over time as the schema matures.

The self-model (#15) rides the same machinery: an `Agent`-authority path lets the agent append self-*observations* that feed a regenerable description, while the operator-fixed charter and voice draw only from `bootstrap`/`Operator` entries — the persona cannot drift even as the description evolves. A self-observation is an attestation by a known-but-fallible teller (the agent), revisable under the same credence and revision machinery as any other belief, and behind the same wall (the load-bearing voice is protected architectural data the agent cannot overwrite). This is the Soar/ACT-R metadata wall applied reflexively.

The road not taken, named explicitly (lane `survey-giants.md`): MemGPT/Letta converged on giving the *model* direct read/write control over its own front-of-mind state via ordinary tool calls — no separate deterministic materialisation layer. It is a live, validated design, not a strawman. zuihitsu deliberately keeps the neural writer separate from a deterministic materialisation layer (descriptions, briefs, visibility) precisely because failure class 11 flags the unverified writer, and the MemGPT family's own evolution — toward more agent-authored state — comes with the same "who checks the model's edits" problem zuihitsu is trying to avoid. Naming it as the road not taken is honest: the redesign chooses checked-and-deterministic curation over model-curates-itself, and the cost of that choice is more substrate machinery and less agent autonomy over its own salience.

## 8. Honest costs, open questions, and where the lanes disagreed

### 8.1 Costs the proposal must own

- **The writer's burden shifts, it does not vanish.** The LLM now emits structure, not prose. The evidence says schema-guidance lowers its error rate, but role assignment above agent/patient is genuinely hard even for expert annotators, so the universal role set must stay small and everything else must be an attribute. The gloss is the safety net when the structure is wrong. (lane `fact-shape.md`)
- **Every fact is now a small graph.** More nodes, edges, materialisation cost, and query surface. Consolation: the encoding syntax is not where the cost concentrates once the graph is genuinely n-ary. (lane `fact-shape.md`)
- **The Constraint Tax.** Schema-forcing can suppress tool-calling or degrade reasoning in some models. The move is to constrain the load-bearing writes and leave the generative parts free, with an eval per behaviour to confirm the constraint paid for itself. This is a live tension with the fact model's ambition. (lane `welding.md`)
- **Cyc's economics.** Any mechanism whose marginal cost per fact does not fall as the log grows imports Cyc's slow death. Per-fact LLM extraction plus critics adds cost; the mitigations are the episodic fallback tier (not every utterance needs full extraction — raw experience is retained cheaply and structured lazily), coupling that reuses signals already computed, and the exception-queue model that keeps human/operator cost sub-linear. This constraint should be stated as a design invariant and measured. (lane `survey-giants.md`)
- **Re-derivation cost on severance and recalibration.** Severing a merge re-runs the voided derivations for the two separated stubs; recalibration on `EmbeddingModelChanged` re-fits thresholds and may re-embed. Both are bounded by the number of derivations touched, not the whole log, but the "cheap in practice" claim is empirical and unverified. (lane `identity-belief.md`, flagged medium confidence)

### 8.2 Open questions

- **Working-scratchpad storage** (#59): log-with-compaction versus a side-table, trading replay-purity against log size. The time-memory lane leans log-with-compaction but flags it as its weakest recommendation. Genuinely unresolved.
- **The exact credence shape.** Subjective logic (richest, best-motivated for the recitation defence, heaviest) versus a lighter (strength, confidence) pair or a categorical decision. The lanes disagree (see 8.3). The specific fusion operator, if any, needs validation against the "Can we trust subjective logic" critique before it is relied upon.
- **The tractable Allen subset — now resolved by the verification pass.** ORD-Horn is the unique maximal tractable subclass of Allen's algebra containing all basic relations (Nebel & Bürckert, JACM 1995); the design question narrows from "which subset" to whether the simpler pointisable subalgebra already covers the anchors the agent actually writes.
- **The assumption-stamp representation.** Per-event set versus a shared assumption-environment table referenced by id — a storage-versus-fold-time trade the identity-belief lane did not resolve.
- **Crumble/accretion thresholds for tentative merges.** An empirical tuning problem needing data, because genuine same-person profiles also diverge. Flagged by #94 and the lane.
- **How much to structure eagerly versus lazily.** Mem0's ADD/UPDATE/DELETE/NOOP at write time versus zuihitsu's defer-to-maintenance-pass model; the survey suggests moving *some* dedup judgement in-line (the ADD-vs-NOOP distinction is cheap at write time), but how much, against the Constraint Tax, is open.

### 8.3 Where the lanes disagreed, recorded

- **Credence shape** (3.5): identity-belief argues for subjective logic; survey-giants counsels a deliberate choice among three tested shapes and notes SL's fusion critics. Resolution offered: adopt SL's representation and discounting, be conservative on fusion operators, keep a lighter fallback in reach.
- **Failure class 4's generality** (3.6): time-memory treats schedule/description conflation as a general design problem with a mature iCalendar answer; survey-issue7 found no peer has the failure, suggesting it is zuihitsu-specific. Resolution: the fix is sound regardless, but the problem's universality should not be oversold.
- **Model-curates-own-context** (6): survey-giants names MemGPT's model-authority-over-salience as a validated road not taken, in tension with zuihitsu's deterministic-materialisation fixed point. Resolution: the redesign keeps checked-and-deterministic curation and names the autonomy cost.
- **Files-versus-database and grep-versus-embeddings** (lane `survey-issue7.md`): the leaner peer projects chose markdown-as-memory with agentic grep, arguing exact-match self-corrects where a single embedding lookup does not. This does not contradict zuihitsu's event-log-is-truth model, but it is a live external data point that embedding-geometry hygiene is not the only defensible retrieval basis — reinforcing the move to make retrieval multi-signal (activation plus structure plus cosine) rather than cosine-first.
- **Whether a general reasoning layer is worth building** (lane `survey-giants.md`, OpenCog/PLN): the graveyard lesson cautions against over-investing in a general inference engine over the richer ontology — the storage-and-bookkeeping layer is what survived in the most comparable prior systems. The proposal deliberately keeps reasoning as focused, task-specific model calls plus sound critics, not a general symbolic reasoner, which is consistent with this caution.
